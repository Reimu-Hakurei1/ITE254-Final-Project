The Inspiration

Our project was born from a shared passion for classic Mario games. Two members of our team (Wachaira and Alex) are avid fans of Mario Kart, while Alex has a special love for Mario Forever. We wanted to create something that pays homage to the games we enjoy while introducing modern web technologies.

The goal was to build a web-based version of Mario Forever that retains the nostalgic gameplay while improving the graphics, adding new features, and making it accessible to everyone through modern browsers.
